How It Works

📲 01. Book Your Service

Contact me on WhatsApp to request a blood sample collection. Share your test requirements, location and preferred collection time.

🩸 02. Sample Collection

Your blood sample will be collected at the agreed location and time, with attention to patient comfort, hygiene and proper handling.

🔬 03. Lab Testing & Report

The collected sample will be transferred to a diagnostic laboratory partner for testing. You will receive support with report collection.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How can I book a home collection?

You can book through our Smart Home Collection Booking form or contact us directly on WhatsApp.

❓ Which areas do you serve?

Home collection is available in selected service areas including Kashimnagar, Hayatnagar, Paikar, Kutubpur and nearby locations. Contact us to confirm availability.

❓ Is home collection free?

No separate home collection charge is offered where the service is available. Applicable laboratory and test charges should be confirmed before booking.

❓ Where will my blood sample be tested?

Samples are transferred to diagnostic laboratory partners for the requested tests.

❓ How can I receive my report?

We provide assistance with report collection. Report availability and delivery time depend on the laboratory and the test.

❓ How can I contact you?

You can contact us directly through WhatsApp for booking, service availability and assistance.
